Jollof Rice

Description

This sweet Jollof is calm and tasty. It is cooked on low to medium low heat in a regular pot and focuses more on the natural sweetness from tomatoes and onions. It works well with sella basmati rice or long grain parboiled rice. There is no aggressive heat or heavy burning involved, which makes it great for everyday meals.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Seasoning
  • Oil
  • Onions
  • Beef Broth
  • Bay leaves
  • Tomato Paste

Steps

  1. Wash the tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, and scotch bonnet. Remove the seeds from the bell peppers. Cut the tomatoes into quarters. Cut the bell peppers and onions into chunks. Place everything in a wide baking dish. If you like garlic, cut a whole bulb in half and place half cut side down so it does not burn. Add fresh or dried thyme, Drizzle the veggies with a little oil.
  2. Cut the beef into chunks and add to a pot. Season with salt, curry powder, bouillon, thyme, and a handful of sliced onions. Pour in a little water about ¼ cup mix then cover and allow to cook.
  3. Pour some oil into a large pot and let it heat up. Add the cooked beef and fry until brown. You can also air fry the beef if you want a lighter option
  4. Add your chopped onions to the pot and fry for a few minutes until they soften. Then add the tomato paste. Keep stirring and let the tomato paste fry until it darkens
  5. Pour the washed rice into the pot and stir gently so every grain is coated in the sauce. Add the remaining water. The water level should be just slightly above the rice.
  6. After about 35 to 40 minutes, check on the rice. The sauce will rise to the top, so stir gently to mix everything together. At this point the rice should be soft, fluffy, just perfect.
